Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit our revised manuscript entitled "Teacher-Distilled
Lightweight Graph Learning for Real-Time Dynamic Job Shop Scheduling" for
consideration in Computers & Industrial Engineering.

**Background and motivation.** Job shop scheduling (JSS) is a core decision
problem in manufacturing systems, where dispatching rules remain the
industrial standard because they are fast and interpretable. However, no
single rule performs consistently across shop configurations, dynamic
arrivals, and production-line reconfigurations. Learning-based dispatch has
shown promise, but many methods assume fixed topologies and rely on
reinforcement learning signals that are slow or unstable to train.

**What we propose.** We develop a Heterogeneous Graph Neural Network (HGNN)
policy that is first trained on CP-SAT optimal trajectories for small shops,
then distilled from schedules generated by the official open-source L2D method
on 30 Taillard instances. The graph is constructed from the current instance
size, so a single trained policy can be applied to unseen shop sizes without
padding or retraining.

**Key findings.** Across 50 held-out 6x5 instances, the policy improves
makespan by 9.1% over SPT (p < 0.0001, 41/50 wins). On 49 official JSPLIB
instances, the distilled policy improves over SPT on all 49 instances with a
mean improvement of 25.2%, beats the best-of-five rule oracle on 33 of 49
instances, and reduces the mean known-best gap from 56.9% to 34.0%. The policy
remains robust under dynamic arrivals (p = 0.021) and machine failures
(p = 0.033). On 37 official instances with matching released L2D checkpoints, the lightweight student remains on average 6.9% behind published L2D, which we report explicitly as the main remaining limitation.

**Why CAIE.** The work addresses industrial engineering concerns of adaptive
scheduling, benchmark rigor, and deployment feasibility. The corrected JSPLIB
parser, teacher schedules, distilled checkpoint, per-instance results, and
benchmark scripts are released to support reproducibility.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not
